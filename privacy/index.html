Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Dinkli, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Account credentials (username, password)
  • Profile information (skill level, location preferences)
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Game preferences and booking history
  • Game video and performance data (stats, coaching notes and Dinkli Rating) for games recorded with Record & Analyze (see Section 4)

Automatically Collected Information

When you use our app, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (device type, operating system, unique device identifiers)
  • Location data (with your permission, to find games near you)
  • Usage data (app features accessed, games booked, time spent in app)
  • Log data (IP address, access times, app crashes)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Process your game bookings and payments
  • Record, analyze and deliver game video, stats, coaching notes and Dinkli Ratings for recorded games
  • Match you with games and players based on skill level and location
  • Send you notifications about game updates, confirmations, and reminders
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send you marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and security threats
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your profile information (name, skill level) may be visible to other players in games you join
  • Service Providers: We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf (payment processing, analytics, cloud hosting)
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred

4. Game Recording and AI Analysis

What we record

When Record & Analyze is added to a booked game, Dinkli's crew records video of that game with a courtside camera. We do not record audio.

How we analyze it

We use automated computer vision (object detection and tracking) to locate players, paddles and the ball in each frame, and to produce game video, stats, coaching notes and your Dinkli Rating. We do not use facial recognition, and we do not create face templates or other biometric identifiers. Players are matched to their position on court using booking information.

Notice and consent

Players are told at booking when a game will be recorded, and recorded courts display Dinkli signage. By booking or joining a recorded game, you agree to be recorded and analyzed as described here. If you do not want to be recorded, choose a game without Record & Analyze or contact us before the game.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own game video, coaching notes, stats and Dinkli Rating.
  • Game video shows everyone on court, so your film may include other players in the same game, and theirs may include you.
  • Host courts may receive aggregate information about recorded games at their facility. Any other use of footage by a host court is agreed in advance and follows this policy.
  • We do not sell game video or performance data.

Retention

We delete raw game video 90 days after your game video is delivered. Your stats, coaching notes and Dinkli Rating history are kept while your account is active and are deleted when you delete your account. You can ask us to delete your game video sooner at any time through our Account Deletion page or the contact form.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access and receive a copy of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information, including game video and performance data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking
  • Request account deletion (see our Account Deletion Policy)

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
