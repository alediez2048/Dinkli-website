Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to change our current booking software?
No. Start with one session. Dinkli is not a replacement for what you already use.
How do ratings work?
After each game, players rate each other's skill, so matchups stay even. Not a camera or AI rating.
Is recording included?
Not today. A phone-upload recording add-on is coming soon; see the homepage. Dinkli does not install cameras.
