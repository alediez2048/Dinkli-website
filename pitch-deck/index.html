Every game recorded. Your courts at the center.

Full-stack platform

One platform runs the court and the player experience.

Dinkli is not a booking widget. It is the whole player journey, from reservation to recorded game to rating, and the operator tools behind it.

Operator problem

Booking tools are interchangeable. Players get nothing back.

Commodity

Any app can reserve a court.

Booking alone gives players no reason to choose your courts over the next facility with open slots.

No feedback

Players can't see their own game.

Most players never watch film of themselves or see real stats. Improvement is guesswork, and guesswork doesn't build loyalty.

Admin

The back office is patched together.

Schedules, memberships, programs, cancellations and communication often live in disconnected systems.

Market signal

Players want better tools, and video is how they want to improve.

Playtomic shows demand for digital sports booking, yet its reviews still call out ratings, no-show controls, filtering and missing functionality. AI video apps exist too, but players have to film and upload themselves. Dinkli brings the crew and plugs the film into booking.

Top feature

Every player gets their own AI game analytics.

Players add Record & Analyze when they book. After the game, every player on court gets their own cut.

  • Personal video with highlight clips.
  • Timestamped coaching notes on what to fix next.
  • Game stats compared with their last game.
  • A Dinkli Rating that moves up or down after every analyzed game.
How the AI works

Crew-recorded. Computer-vision analyzed.

  1. BookPlayer adds Record & Analyze.
  2. Set upOur crew mounts the rig courtside.
  3. DetectYOLO finds every player, paddle and ball.
  4. AnalyzePositioning, shots, errors, rallies.
  5. DeliverFilm, notes, stats and rating in the app.
Under the hood

Detection. YOLO object detection identifies each player, paddle and the ball in every frame of video.

Tracking. Detections are linked frame to frame, so every player is followed through the whole game.

Privacy. No audio. No face recognition. Players are matched to their tracks through their booking.

No hardware for you. The crew brings the rig and takes it away. Nothing to install or buy.
