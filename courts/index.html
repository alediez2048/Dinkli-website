Recording Without the Hardware

Our crew. Our cameras. Your courts.

When a player books Record & Analyze, a Dinkli crew member arrives before the game, mounts the camera rig courtside and packs it up afterwards.

  • No install and no equipment to buy
  • No staff time from your team
  • Works with the courts you already have
  • Recorded courts are marked with Dinkli signage
Under the hood

Detection. YOLO object detection identifies each player, paddle and the ball in every frame of video.

Tracking. Detections are linked frame to frame, so every player is followed through the whole game.

Privacy. No audio. No face recognition. Players are matched to their tracks through their booking.

Host Court Questions

How much space does the camera rig need?
The rig stands on a tripod just outside the court, usually at a corner behind the baseline. The crew places it so it stays out of play and out of walkways.
Do we need to provide Wi-Fi or power?
No. The crew brings what the rig needs and takes it away after the game.
How do players consent to being recorded?
Players are notified when they book a recorded game, and recorded courts are marked with Dinkli signage. Dinkli records video only, with no audio and no facial recognition. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
Who owns the footage?
Players get their own game video and stats. How footage can be used by your court is agreed with you before the pilot starts, and every use follows our Privacy Policy.
How does revenue on Record & Analyze work?
Host courts share in Record & Analyze revenue. We agree the share with you during the pilot, alongside how bookings and memberships are priced.
Do we have to switch everything to Dinkli on day one?
No. Start with one session or league for 30 days, see how players respond, and expand from there.
