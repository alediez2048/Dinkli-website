The Idea

You run games at private clubs. Instead of group chats and Venmo requests, Dinkli lists your game at the price you set, players book and pay in the app, and Dinkli sends you your share after the game.

We lead with private clubs that sell court time. Paid games at City of Austin parks count as commercial activity and need a PARD permit; Dinkli does not list paid park games without a permit. Captains are responsible for venue rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get paid?
Players pay in the app, and Dinkli sends you your share after the game.
Do I set the price?
Yes. Dinkli lists your game at your price.
How do ratings work?
After each game, players rate each other's skill, so matchups stay even.
Can I run a paid game at a City of Austin park?
Only with a PARD permit. We lead with private clubs.
