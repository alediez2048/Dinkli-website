Under the hood

Installed cameras. Dinkli cameras are mounted permanently at your courts. Nothing for your staff or players to carry or set up.

Detection. YOLO object detection identifies each player, paddle and the ball in every frame of video.

Tracking. Detections are linked frame to frame, so every player is followed from the opening serve to the final point.

Privacy. No audio. No face recognition. Players are matched to their tracks through their booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does Dinkli install?
Two things: the Dinkli booking platform for your courts, sessions, payments and memberships, and AI cameras mounted at your courts. We handle the install and set everything up with your team.
What do the cameras need from our facility?
We plan mounting, power and connectivity with you during a site visit before the install, so the cameras stay out of play and out of the way.
Is every game recorded?
No. Games are recorded only when a player adds Record & Analyze to their booking. Everyone in that game is notified at booking, and recorded courts are marked with signage.
What is YOLO, in plain English?
YOLO ("You Only Look Once") is a computer vision model that looks at a frame of video and draws a box around every object it recognizes. In a Dinkli game, that means each player, paddle and the ball. We run it on every frame, then link those boxes from frame to frame to follow each player through the whole game. Our analysis turns that movement into stats, coaching notes and the Dinkli Rating.
Is audio recorded? Do you use facial recognition?
No and no. Dinkli records video only, and players are matched to their tracks through their booking, not their faces. See our Privacy Policy for how game video is handled.
How does revenue on Record & Analyze work?
Record & Analyze is a paid add-on players choose per game, and host courts share in it. We agree the share with you during the pilot, alongside how bookings and memberships are priced.
Is the Dinkli Rating the same as DUPR?
No. The Dinkli Rating is Dinkli's own rating and isn't affiliated with DUPR.
Do we have to switch everything on day one?
No. Start with one session or league for 30 days, see how your players respond, and expand from there.
I'm a player. How do I use Dinkli?
Book through the Dinkli app at any Dinkli court. Want Dinkli where you play? Tell your court, or send us their details.
