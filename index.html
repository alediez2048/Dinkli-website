How It Works

1

Get the app

Download Dinkli on iOS and create an account.

2

Find a game

Browse hosted games in Explore, time, place, skill level, and price.

3

Book a spot

Reserve and pay in the app. The game shows up in your reservations.

4

Show up and play

Show up at the time and place. Play pickleball.

5

Rate players

After the game, rate the people you played with. Ratings come from players on court, not from a camera or an AI score.

Find Regular Games

Games can run on a regular day and time, so you can play with the same crowd week to week. Each game is listed with a skill level. This is not an automatic matching engine.

Browse Games

Find games by time, place, and skill level.

Your Reservations

All your upcoming games in one place.

Ratings

Players rate each other

After each game, players rate each other's skill, so matchups stay even. Ratings come from the players on court, not a camera or an AI score, and are not affiliated with DUPR.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Dinkli?
A pickleball booking app. Find a hosted game, book a spot, pay, and show up. After each game, players rate each other's skill, so matchups stay even.
How do I book?
Get the iOS app, browse Explore, and book a spot.
Is it on Android?
Not yet.
How do ratings work?
After each game, players rate each other's skill, so matchups stay even. Not a camera or AI score, and not DUPR.
Where are the games?
Hosted games are currently run in Austin. Check Explore in the app.
How much does a game cost?
Each game lists its price in the app.
