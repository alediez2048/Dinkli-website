Under the hood

Detection. YOLO object detection identifies each player, paddle and the ball in every frame of video.

Tracking. Detections are linked frame to frame, so every player is followed from the opening serve to the final point.

Privacy. No audio. No face recognition. Players are matched to their tracks through their booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the camera setup work?
When you add Record & Analyze to a booking, a Dinkli crew member arrives before your game, mounts the camera rig courtside and handles everything. There's nothing to install, charge or carry. When the game ends, we pack up and your footage goes to processing.
What is YOLO, in plain English?
YOLO ("You Only Look Once") is a computer vision model that looks at a frame of video and draws a box around every object it recognizes. In a Dinkli game, that means each player, paddle and the ball. We run it on every frame, then link those boxes from frame to frame to follow each player through the whole game. Our analysis turns that movement into your stats, coaching notes and Dinkli Rating.
What about other players on court?
Every player in a recorded game is notified when they book, and recorded courts are marked with signage. Each player gets their own cut, stats and notes. See our Privacy Policy for how game video is handled.
Is audio recorded?
No. Dinkli records video only, and we don't use facial recognition. Players are matched to their tracks through their booking, not their faces.
How fast do I get my video?
After your game is processed, the app notifies you when your video, coaching notes and stats are ready.
How does the Dinkli Rating work?
Your Dinkli Rating is built from what happens in your analyzed games: positioning, shot selection, errors and rally outcomes. It moves up or down after every analyzed game, and your stats show what changed it.
Is this DUPR?
No. The Dinkli Rating is Dinkli's own rating and isn't affiliated with DUPR.
Can I still just book a court?
Yes. Dinkli is a full booking platform for courts, open play, clinics, leagues, memberships and passes. Record & Analyze is an add-on you choose game by game.
